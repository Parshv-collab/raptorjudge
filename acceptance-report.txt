Scope

This report reflects the complete portability pass (PART A / PART G), full T1–T4 feature completion & bug fixes (PART B through PART L), and additional UI/UX & issue fixes (ISSUES 15–26).

PART A — Portability & Runtime Config: PASS
--------------------------------------------
- Vite defines `__CONVEX_URL__` as a build-time placeholder.
- `src/main.tsx` dynamically evaluates `__CONVEX_URL__` or falls back to `import.meta.env.VITE_CONVEX_URL`.
- `frontend/entrypoint.sh` substitutes `CONVEX_URL` into JS assets and `index.html` at container startup before launching nginx.
- `docker-compose.yml` backend service includes `CONVEX_CLOUD_ORIGIN`, `CONVEX_SITE_ORIGIN`, and `TRUSTED_ORIGINS`.
- `src/convex/http.ts` merges `TRUSTED_ORIGINS` with default origins (`http://localhost:3000`, `http://127.0.0.1:3000`).
- Hardcoded URLs replaced across scripts; `README.md` updated with "Deploying Anywhere" instructions.

PART G — Auto-Generate JWT Keys on First Boot: PASS
--------------------------------------------------
- `backend/entrypoint.sh` invokes `scripts/generate-auth-keys.mjs` to auto-mint RS256 keypairs when `JWT_PRIVATE_KEY` and `JWKS` are missing from the deployment.
