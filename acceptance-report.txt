1. BUG ANALYSIS & FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Issue: Post-login redirection previously sent all users to "/" regardless of role because Auth.tsx called navigate(returnTo, { replace: true }) where returnTo defaulted to "/".
- Fix: Modified src/pages/Auth.tsx to read the authenticated state and user role via useConvexAuth() and useQuery(api.users.me). Included a useEffect hook guarded by isAuthenticated and me that redirects users to their designated role path:
  * admin       → /organizer
  * organizer   → /organizer
  * judge       → /judge
  * participant → /workspace
