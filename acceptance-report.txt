T3 — Public Features (7/7 PASS)
- T3.1 Configurable community voting: PASS (Plain upvote and quadratic voting with credit budget caps in voting.ts, ProjectDetail.tsx, and OrganizerEventManage.tsx).
- T3.2 Comments: PASS (Add comment, flag for moderation, user deletion, organizer moderation in comments.ts and OrganizerEventManage.tsx).
- T3.3 Hidden results during voting: PASS (Vote counts, scores, and rankings hidden until voting closes / published, with organizer live preview badge).
- T3.4 Randomized project ordering: PASS (Deterministic mulberry32 seeded PRNG shuffle in src/lib/shuffle.ts and Gallery.tsx with organizer toggle).
- T3.5 Rate limiting: PASS (Server-side rate limit buckets for signup, login, voting, comments, and submission edits in rateLimit.ts with clean 429 messages).
- T3.6 Duplicate detection: PASS (Flag duplicate title or repo URL for organizer review in submissions.ts, flags table, and OrganizerEventManage.tsx).
- T3.7 Audit trail chain verification: PASS (SHA-256 hash chain verification in audit.ts verifyChain and AdminAudit.tsx).
