WHAT THIS SHOWS
  30 judges scored the same fixture population on a 1–5 rubric. They do not
  agree on the scale. The most generous panel
  (jdg_02 Wei Lindqvist) averaged 4.222; the harshest
  (jdg_01 Tomas Varga) averaged 2.000 — a 2.222-point gap on
  a five-point scale. A raw average therefore measures *who happened to review
  you*. Per-judge z-score normalization removes the panel before the ranking is
  taken; the sections below show the effect on the real 41-project corpus, and the
  judge-mean spread collapses from 0.420 to 0.000.

HOW TO REGENERATE
  npm run proof:normalization
  (transpiles and calls the real src/lib/algorithms/normalization.ts, so these
   numbers cannot drift from the app)

Formula
  per (judge, project):  raw = Σ(criterion_score × criterion_weight) / Σ(criterion_weight)
  z-score:               z = (raw − judge_mean) / judge_stddev
  ten-point scale:       normalized = clamp(5 + z × 2, 0, 10)
  degenerate case:       judge_stddev = 0  →  min-max: (raw − lo) / (hi − lo) × 10
